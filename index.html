Who We Are

Built around experienced drivers.

CIA Transport is a flatbed specialized trucking company based in Joliet, IL area, running ~40 trucks all 48 states, moving construction equipment and other specialized freight that require driver attention to details. We built this company on experienced drivers, and we pay like it.
